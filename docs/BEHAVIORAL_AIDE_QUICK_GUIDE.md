# Behavioral Aide Quick Guide

## Before a hard moment

Offer predictability, communication choices, movement access, and sensory options before assuming misconduct.

## During a hard moment

- use fewer words;
- reduce unnecessary demands;
- protect physical safety;
- preserve dignity and privacy;
- offer familiar choices;
- allow processing time;
- do not argue about whether the learner “should” be overwhelmed.

## After

Record context, observable actions, support offered, and what happened next. Avoid motives and labels.

## The key question

**What can we change so this student has better access next time?**
