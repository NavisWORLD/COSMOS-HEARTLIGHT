# HEARTLIGHT Study Arc

A 10-session professional-learning course for teachers, paraprofessionals, behavior aides, therapists, librarians, coaches, substitutes, and family partners.

## Session 1 — The overlooked learner
Goal: distinguish inability, overload, communication difference, processing time, and lack of access from simplistic “won't do it” narratives.

Practice: rewrite five judgmental observations as neutral descriptions.

## Session 2 — Sensory environments
Goal: map light, sound, movement, crowding, texture, predictability, and task complexity.

Practice: perform a classroom sensory walk-through without identifying any child.

## Session 3 — Communication is broader than speech
Goal: recognize AAC, gesture, typing, drawing, pointing, echolalia/repetition, behavior, and silence as possible parts of communication.

Practice: offer the same classroom choice through three channels.

## Session 4 — Stimming and regulation
Goal: stop treating repetition as automatically meaningless or inappropriate.

Practice: classify scenarios into safe/neutral, needs environmental support, or immediate physical safety concern.

## Session 5 — COSMOS/CST support loop
Goal: use perceive -> compress -> expand -> validate -> express -> store.

Practice: generate three competing support hypotheses before acting.

## Session 6 — The transparent 12D classroom state
Goal: understand HEARTLIGHT's 12 support variables as a temporary situation map, not a child score.

Practice: change one input and inspect exactly how the state changes.

## Session 7 — Observation without surveillance
Goal: collect minimum useful information.

Practice: remove names, medical speculation, and irrelevant details from sample notes.

## Session 8 — Positive proactive support
Goal: connect classroom practice to a tiered, positive support framework and individualized planning.

Practice: build a universal support, targeted support, and individualized support example without using punishment as the organizing principle.

## Session 9 — Accessibility and autonomy
Goal: design interfaces, instructions, and environments with multiple ways to perceive, act, communicate, and regulate.

Practice: test HEARTLIGHT with keyboard-only navigation, large text, high contrast, and reduced motion.

## Session 10 — Validation and humility
Goal: treat every support as a hypothesis.

Practice: design an A/B classroom support test that measures access and learner-reported usefulness without withholding necessary supports.

## Completion project

Create a one-page support plan containing:

- learner-preferred communication options;
- known environmental barriers;
- preferred regulation/stimming options;
- transition supports;
- task-access supports;
- what adults should avoid;
- how the learner can reject or change a support;
- data-retention period;
- team review date.
