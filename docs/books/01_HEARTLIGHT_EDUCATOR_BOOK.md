# HEARTLIGHT — Educator Book
## A practical field guide for teachers, aides, families, and support teams

### Purpose

HEARTLIGHT begins with a simple rule: behavior can carry information, and support should begin with observation, communication, access, and dignity before assumptions.

### Chapter 1 — See the learner before the label

Describe what happened in observable terms. "Covered ears when the bell rang" is useful. "Was defiant" assigns motive. HEARTLIGHT is built to make the first kind of description easier.

### Chapter 2 — Give communication more than one door

Speech is one communication channel, not the definition of communication. A learner may point, type, gesture, select a card, use AAC, draw, move away, or say "I don't know." The tool treats those as information rather than failure.

### Chapter 3 — Sensory access

Light, sound, movement, proximity, transitions, and predictability can change whether learning is accessible. The goal is not a universally "calm" classroom. The goal is an environment with choices and a path back to learning.

### Chapter 4 — Stimming

A repetitive movement or sound can be regulation, communication, focus, expression, or joy. Start with safety and access, not suppression. If a stim is harmless, the burden is on the environment to justify interference.

### Chapter 5 — The HEARTLIGHT loop

```text
observe -> summarize -> generate support possibilities -> ask/check -> try -> observe again
```

No step authorizes diagnosis or punishment.

### Chapter 6 — Use the app in under five minutes

1. Open the Child tab.
2. Let the learner select needs without pressure.
3. Review gentle options.
4. Offer, do not force.
5. If an observation is worth keeping, use a non-identifying student code and save only the minimum needed.
6. Erase local records when they are no longer required.

### Chapter 7 — Team conversations

Use HEARTLIGHT notes to ask better questions: What conditions were present? What did the learner communicate? What support was tried? What changed? What does the learner say helped?

### Chapter 8 — Boundaries

HEARTLIGHT is not a diagnosis engine, medical device, crisis service, restraint protocol, IEP authority, or automated discipline system. Established school and emergency procedures remain authoritative.
