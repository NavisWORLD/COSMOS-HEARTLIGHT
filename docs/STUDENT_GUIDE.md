# HEARTLIGHT Student Guide

This tool is here to help you tell people what might make learning easier.

You can choose things like:

- I need quiet.
- I need to move.
- The light is too much.
- I need more space.
- I need help.
- I need a break.
- Tell me what happens next.
- Let me communicate another way.
- I don't know.
- Stay with me.

You do not have to pick anything you do not want to pick.

The app is not grading your feelings. There is no “good” or “bad” score. The 12 bars on the teacher screen describe the current situation; they do not define who you are.

The camera color tool is supposed to look at the room or light, not your face.

You can tell an adult that a suggested support does not help. Your answer matters more than the app's suggestion.
