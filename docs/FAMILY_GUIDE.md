# 💚 HEARTLIGHT Family Guide

## What is this?

HEARTLIGHT is an open-source classroom support tool that gives a learner another way to communicate needs such as quiet, movement, a break, less light, more space, help, predictability, or another way to communicate.

It also gives teachers a structured way to record **what they observed, what support they tried, and what happened next**.

## What it does not do

HEARTLIGHT does not diagnose autism, ADHD, sensory processing conditions, anxiety, intellectual disability, communication disorders, or any other medical/educational condition.

It does not determine an IEP, placement, discipline consequence, medication, restraint, seclusion, or eligibility decision.

## Why the app has an “I don’t know” button

A learner should not have to invent an answer just because an adult asked a question. “I don’t know” is valid communication and may mean the learner needs time, fewer choices, or simply does not know what would help yet.

## Does it record my child through the camera?

The reference app's optional camera feature is designed to sample **ambient room color and brightness**. It should be pointed at the room/light source rather than the learner. Frames are not intentionally retained by the application.

Schools should follow their own device, camera, consent, and privacy policies.

## Where is information stored?

The public reference build has no cloud account or analytics service. Learner check-ins are session state. Adult observation notes are saved only when the adult presses Save, and they remain in the browser's local storage unless exported.

A school can modify the software, so families should ask the school what version it uses and whether any additional storage/service has been added.

## Questions families can ask a school

- Why is HEARTLIGHT being used with my child?
- Is use optional?
- What information is saved?
- Where is it saved?
- Who can see it?
- How long is it kept?
- How can it be corrected or deleted?
- Is the school using the unmodified local-first reference version?
- Does the school use the camera sensor?
- Can my child use the communication features without the camera?
- How will my child's own preferences be included in decisions?

## The goal

The goal is not to make every learner look or behave the same.

The goal is to make communication easier, identify environmental barriers, and help adults try supports that improve access to learning while preserving dignity and autonomy.
