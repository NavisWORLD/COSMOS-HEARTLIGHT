# HEARTLIGHT Clinician / Therapy-Adjunct Usage Guide

## Scope

HEARTLIGHT may be used alongside appropriately licensed or credentialed school-based professionals as a **communication, observation, accessibility, and environmental-support aid**. It is not itself therapy and does not implement a clinical treatment protocol.

Potential users include occupational therapists, speech-language pathologists, school psychologists, counselors, behavior specialists, nurses, special educators, and interdisciplinary teams, within their own scopes of practice.

## Appropriate uses

- let a learner report sensory/environmental needs;
- compare environmental conditions with learner-reported comfort;
- document support attempts in neutral language;
- support collaborative goal discussion;
- test environmental accommodations;
- provide a visual timer or transition aid;
- help a learner communicate whether an option helped.

## Inappropriate uses

- diagnosis or screening without a validated instrument and qualified professional;
- medical interpretation of camera/color readings;
- heart-rate diagnosis or autonomic-state inference;
- psychotherapy recommendations generated from the 12D state;
- crisis assessment;
- suicide/self-harm risk prediction;
- violence prediction;
- restraint/seclusion decisions;
- replacement of an FBA, IEP team, clinical assessment, or emergency evaluation.

## Suggested adjunct workflow

1. Obtain the consent/authorization required by the setting.
2. Define a narrow question: e.g., “Does reduced glare improve task access during independent reading?”
3. Use the child's preferred communication channel to record baseline comfort/access.
4. Change one environmental/support variable when feasible.
5. Ask the child again and record an observable outcome.
6. Repeat across enough occasions to avoid conclusions from a single day.
7. Review with the child and team.
8. Keep, modify, or discard the support based on usefulness and safety.

## Camera and pulse boundary

The ambient sensor is for **room/light sampling**. Point it at the environment, not a child. Frames are processed in memory and not intentionally persisted by the supplied app.

The optional pulse field is manual entry only. The app does not calculate pulse, diagnose arousal, or infer emotion from heart rate.

## Documentation rule

Use the minimum necessary data. Prefer pseudonymous student codes. Do not copy clinical records into HEARTLIGHT unless the school has specifically approved that workflow.
