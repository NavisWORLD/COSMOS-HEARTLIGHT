# Contributing

Contributions are welcome when they preserve HEARTLIGHT's core child-safety boundaries.

## Required for contributions

- no covert surveillance;
- no ads or child profiling;
- no automated diagnosis, discipline, restraint, seclusion, or risk scoring;
- sensors must be optional and fail safely;
- explainable mappings over opaque labels;
- preserve learner choice and rejection;
- document every new persistent data field;
- include erase/export behavior;
- add tests for privacy-sensitive functionality.

Clinical/therapeutic claims require appropriate evidence and must be clearly separated from exploratory hypotheses.
